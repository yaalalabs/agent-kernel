Sarasavi Power — Privacy Policy

WhatsApp assistant for household electricity bill estimates in Sri Lanka. Last updated: 30 August 2026.

What this service is

Sarasavi Power is a WhatsApp assistant that estimates a household's electricity consumption and bill using the published CEB/LECO domestic tariff, and suggests ways to reduce it. All figures are estimates, not official CEB or LECO bills.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number — used only as the identifier for your session.
  • Household details you choose to give — appliances, daily usage hours, billing cycle, preferred language, and any meter/bill units you type.
  • Message and call content — text, voice notes, and voice call audio are processed to produce a reply.

We do not collect your name, address, ID number, payment details, or your actual CEB account.

Consent

No household details are stored until you explicitly agree when asked. You may refuse and still use the service; it will simply not remember between messages.

How your data is used

Only to answer you. Your messages, voice notes, and call audio are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies. All electricity calculations are performed locally by a deterministic engine, not by the AI model. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or any third party beyond what is needed to deliver the reply.

Storage and retention

Your household profile is stored in an access-controlled Amazon DynamoDB table and is automatically deleted after 7 days of inactivity. Voice call audio is not recorded or retained after the call ends.

Your rights

At any time, send the assistant a message asking to:

  • Export your stored data — it will be shown back to you;
  • Delete your stored data — it is erased immediately;
  • Withdraw consent — this erases the stored profile at once.

Safety

The assistant will not give electrical wiring, meter, or repair instructions. Consult a licensed electrician for any physical electrical work.

Contact

For any privacy question or data-deletion request, message the assistant on WhatsApp, or email cybersecurity@bugcircuit.com.
