When `logging.ak.level` or `logging.system.level` is set, OAK's log lines now start with a UTC ISO 8601 timestamp with milliseconds, e.g. `2026-10-21T08:15:12.345Z`, instead of local time in the form `2026-10-21 08:15:12,345`. Request-path log lines also drop their bracketed tags, e.g. `[AGENT START] request_id=…` is now `Agent start: request_id=…`, so only `TRACE` lines carry a `[STAGE]` tag. Update any log parser, search or alert that matched the old timestamp or tags.
