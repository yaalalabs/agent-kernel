Stream tokens over SSE from AWS ECS in queue mode: set `execution.mode: rest_sync` with `execution.message_type: delta` and give the deployment a Redis or Valkey response store, since DynamoDB cannot stream chunks. Two new Terraform inputs bound a stream: `alb_idle_timeout` for a silent gap between chunks, and `gateway_integration_timeout` for its total length. See [AWS containerized](https://kernel.yaala.ai/docs/deployment/aws-containerized).
