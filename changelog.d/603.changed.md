Scheduled tasks: an occurrence whose agent has gone, or whose owner no longer holds access to it, is skipped instead of run. The trigger answers 403 and the task keeps the reason in its new `last_error` field (a custom `ScheduleStore` records it once its `record_trigger` accepts `error`); a task created by a chat request also keeps that request's surface and runs only the agents on it. See [Skipped occurrences](https://kernel.yaala.ai/docs/advanced/scheduling#skipped-occurrences).
